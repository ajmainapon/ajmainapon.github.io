About Me

I am a final-year Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering undergraduate student at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh, expecting to graduate in October 2026.

My research spans self-supervised representation learning, controllable text-to-image generative models, and computer vision benchmarks. I am particularly interested in how self-supervision and training-free guidance can steer neural network representations in low-data regimes.

Currently, I am conducting senior thesis research on steering Latent Diffusion Models via predicted memorability guidance (GenMem), as well as first-author research (under review at WACV 2027) on label-efficient 3D medical CT segmentation via 2.5D Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (I-JEPA) pretraining. Previously, I developed instance-segmentation benchmarks for stomatal phenotyping in a funded research project.

Additionally, I am an active competitive programmer, having solved 500+ algorithmic challenges in C++ on Codeforces (handle: fynm).

Senior Thesis · Active
Senior Thesis — GenMem: Auditing Predicted-Memorability Guidance in Text-to-Image Diffusion
Md. Ajmain Istiak Apon (Under Faculty Supervision)
Department of Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering, University of Dhaka | Jan 2026 – Present
  • Implemented GenMem, a training-free guidance method that differentiates a pretrained memorability predictor (AMNet) through the DDIM sampling trajectory of Stable Diffusion, leaving all model weights frozen.
  • On DrawBench (n=200): held-out memorability rose +3.42% (ResMem) and +2.72% (ViTMem), both p < 10⁻⁸, with no significant CLIP-fidelity loss; replicated on COCO (n=200) with equal or larger effect sizes.
  • Validated causality via reverse-guidance symmetry and a null random-critic control; characterized proxy over-optimization in SDXL and mapped a three-regime dose–response curve across an 8-point guidance-weight sweep.

First-Author Paper · Under Review
Self-Supervised Learning for Label-Efficient Medical CT Segmentation
Md. Ajmain Istiak Apon (First Author)
Submitted / Under Review at WACV 2027 (Algorithms Track) | Jan 2026 – Present
  • Adapted I-JEPA to 2.5D CT slice triplets with a ViT-B/16 backbone and EMA target encoder on 1,228 unlabeled TotalSegmentator volumes; LOO ablation showed natural-image design choices invert on medical CT—narrow slice gap yielded the A3 backbone, improving linear-probe Dice by 44–124%.
  • A3 features significantly outperform random init under frozen and fine-tuned probing at N=20/50/100 labels (Welch t: p=0.017 at N=20, p<0.001 at N=50); achieved 67% recovery of nnU-Net supervised performance at N=50, liver reaching 90%; seed variance reduced 1.3–2.2×.
  • Validated cross-dataset transfer on AMOS22-CT (+158–260% over random init) and cross-modality CT→MR (+41–48% at N=20/50); reproduced collapsing SSL gain on 3D SwinUNETR, confirming cross-architecture robustness.
Stack: PyTorch, timm, MONAI, Nibabel, CUDA
